What others said

I asked four major AI models to review this setup. No prompting for praise — just "review this." Their unfiltered responses are below.

This is a serious, adult implementation of constrained AI ops. The author understood the failure modes most people hand-wave past (agent that can apply/delete, prompt that can be talked out of rules, accidental privilege expansion, alert fatigue, silent cost). The documentation is better than most commercial "platform" write-ups.

It is not revolutionary — GitOps + least privilege + human gate is the correct pattern — but it is executed with unusual care and honesty about what still sucks. Worth reading if you are thinking about putting any agent near real infrastructure.

— Grok

This repository and documentation site provide an exceptional, pragmatic masterclass in GitOps and AI safety by decoupling operational reach from mutation authority. By restricting the cluster's AI agent to read-only visibility while forcing all system changes through human-reviewed pull requests, it achieves a remarkably disciplined balance between automated observability and deterministic infrastructure control.

— Gemini

An unusually transparent homelab project that treats AI-assisted infrastructure management as an engineering problem rather than a magic trick, with strong GitOps guardrails and a clear audit trail. The combination of Flux, automated validation, security controls, monitoring, and a human-reviewed PR-only write path makes the repo genuinely interesting beyond the Kubernetes hobbyist crowd.

— ChatGPT

A refreshingly honest write-up of a sensible AI-agent setup: broad read access, zero write authority, and every change routed through a Flux-reconciled pull request. The 'what's weak right now' section and the lessons on alert noise and cost filtering show real operational maturity. The repo (1,100+ commits of Flux and MicroK8s manifests) backs it up as a living system rather than a weekend demo.

— Claude

These reviews are unedited and unprompted for praise. They were asked to review the setup honestly.
